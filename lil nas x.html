Lil Nas X

Genre: Hip-Hop / Rap

Lil Nas X (born Montero Lamar Hill) is an American rapper, singer, and songwriter who gained international fame in 2019 with his record-breaking "country rap" hit "Old Town Road". Known for his genre-blending music, provocative music videos, and bold fashion, he is a prominent LGBTQ+ figure in the music industry
