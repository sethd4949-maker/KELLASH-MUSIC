A Study Investigating the Role of Music in Organic Coherence and Psychophysical Regulation

Musical Patterns and Bodily Coherence The research examines how specific musical patterns can help generate order and organic coherence, promoting physiological balance when music is applied in therapeutic contexts by trained professionals. The authors suggest that music, beyond its artistic and emotional dimensions, could serve as a tool capable of influencing the functional organisation of the body, paving the way for new lines of research into the relationship between sound, biology, and well-being. Insights from the Authors According to Miguel Ángel Gago Martínez, one of the study’s authors:
“The right music can help create organic coherence and favour psychophysical regulatory processes when applied with therapeutic knowledge.” The proposed approach has generated significant interest in the field of applied musical research in health. Among the feedback received is that of musician and researcher Jordi Jauset, who noted that this perspective represents an original and valuable contribution to the emerging field studying the therapeutic effects of music. Interdisciplinary Approaches to Music and Health The research opens the door to new interdisciplinary approaches that connect music, physiology, neuroscience, and integrative therapies. It highlights the importance of further exploring how specific sound stimuli can contribute to bodily balance and psychophysical well-being. About the Authors Miguel Ángel Gago Martínez researches the relationship between music, organic coherence, and psychophysical regulation from an interdisciplinary perspective connecting sound, biology, and well-being.
